# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Seedshop Template serves people buying seeds online for dachas, greenhouses, balconies, and small home gardens. Many buyers value plain navigation, recognizable categories, trustworthy delivery and payment details, and a checkout that does not require registration.

## Product Purpose

The storefront helps customers discover vegetable, herb, flower, and seed set products, add them to cart, and place orders through a simple ecommerce flow. Success means users can find the right seeds quickly, understand what happens after purchase, and complete checkout with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Warm, clear, grounded. The brand should feel like an organized garden: practical enough for repeat shopping, calm enough for older or less technical customers, and tactile enough to suit the agricultural subject.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS gloss, aggressive marketplace clutter, purple AI gradients, fake precision metrics, decorative complexity, and layouts that make product discovery feel secondary to visual effects.

## Design Principles

1. Keep buying paths obvious.
2. Let real products and category imagery carry the page.
3. Use green as a confident action color, not as a wash over every surface.
4. Prefer calm motion that explains hierarchy or interaction state.
5. Preserve SEO, route structure, nav labels, and the existing Russian copy voice unless a task explicitly asks to change them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls, visible focus states, keyboard-accessible navigation, readable Russian typography, and reduced-motion fallbacks for all decorative or automatic animation.


## Commercial Product Direction

The template is a reusable starting point for individually delivered seed and garden stores. Its quality development proceeds independently of prospect research in LeadForge. Preserve the incumbent design identity while improving buyer journeys, daily seller operations, reliability and adaptation effort.

The working division of responsibilities is that merchants manage everyday catalog and order work in the admin UI. Bespoke design, structure, integrations and behavior changes are services delivered by the implementer. Long-term support is under consideration; subscription terms and a centralized update platform are not decided.

The current product improvement assignment is in docs/IMPROVEMENT-GOAL.md. Client-specific trading terms, legal texts, provider accounts and go-live authorization must be established per implementation. Higher perceived value and price positioning are objectives, not measured claims about conversion or revenue.
